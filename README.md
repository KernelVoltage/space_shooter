# Space Shooter

An action-packed, fast-paced 2D Space Arcade Shooter built using **Godot Engine 4**. Navigate through intense interstellar battlefields, eliminate incoming enemy formations, unleash devastating power-ups, and upgrade your space vessel to conquer the galaxy!

---

## 🌌 Overview & Storyline

Deep in outer space, hostile alien forces and volatile asteroid fields threaten galactic peace. As the pilot of the advanced battle cruiser **Alpha Prime**, you are the final line of defense. Engage in non-stop, intense space combat, dodge deadly enemy laser fire, destroy massive elite bosses, and salvage resources to build the ultimate starship.

---

## 🔥 Key Game Features

### 🎮 Smooth & Dynamic Controls
* **Multi-Platform Controls:** Full support for both Touch/Drag controls for mobile screens and precise Arrow/WASD keyboard controls for desktop devices.
* **Responsive Physics:** Seamless ship handling with smooth acceleration, boundaries clamping, and realistic momentum.
* **Particle Effects:** Dynamic engine thrusters, explosion particle dynamics, and custom impact visuals for an immersive experience.

### 🛡️ Advanced Power-Up System
Enhance your combat capabilities on the fly with dynamic power-ups that spawn during battle:
* **🧲 Magnetic Field (Magnet):** Pulls all surrounding gold coins and drops directly to your ship from anywhere on the screen.
* **🛡️ Energy Shield:** Creates an impenetrable force field around your vessel, neutralizing incoming laser strikes and collision damage.
* **⚡ Energy Overcharge (Rapid Fire):** Dramatically reduces weapon cooldowns, allowing you to unleash a relentless storm of laser fire.
* **⭐ Star Shot (Triple Shot):** Multiplies your laser barrels to fire multiple spreading shots simultaneously across the field.
* **💎 Purple Crystal (Overcharge):** Unlocks hyper-dense multi-beam firepower with customized high-damage laser patterns.
* **⏳ Quantum Clock (Slow Motion):** Warps space-time to drastically slow down enemy movement and projectile speeds, giving you supreme tactical evasion.
* **💚 Nano Repair (Health & Max HP):** Instantly restores lost shield/hull integrity and increases your total health pool.

### 🛸 Enemy Types & Boss Battles
* **Standard Attack Craft:** Fast-moving enemy ships that charge in formations.
* **Zigzag Raiders:** Unpredictable, highly aggressive fighters that maneuver in erratic patterns to collide with your vessel.
* **Heavy Asteroids:** Massive space rocks that require sustained firepower to destroy before they crush your hull.
* **Elite Boss Ships:** Huge enemy command ships equipped with massive health pools, dynamic phase attacks, and dense bullet-hell laser patterns.

### 🛒 In-Game Upgrade Shop
Spend your hard-earned cosmic coins between runs to permanently boost your ship's performance:
* **Laser Damage Upgrades:** Increase the base damage output of your main weapons to shred through high-HP targets faster.
* **Engine Speed Upgrades:** Boost your starship's maneuverability to effortlessly dodge complex enemy fire.
* **Hull Fortification (Max HP):** Expand your maximum hit points to survive longer in late-game stages.

### 🎵 Audio & Custom Settings
* **Audio Mixer Integration:** Full volume controls for both Background Music (BGM) and Sound Effects (SFX) via dedicated audio buses.
* **Interactive UI:** Smooth screen transitions with fade-in/fade-out overlays, custom hover/click animations, and crisp retro-modern menus.

## ⚡ How to Run the Project

1. **Download or Clone** this repository to your local system.
2. Launch **Godot Engine 4.x**.
3. Click **Import**, navigate to the project directory, and select the `project.godot` file.
4. Click **Run / Play Project (F5)** to start the game!
