# Space Shooter

A fast-paced 2D Space Shooter arcade game developed using **Godot Engine 4**. Blast through waves of enemies, collect unique power-ups, upgrade your ship in the shop, and aim for the high score!

---

##  Features

* **Dynamic Gameplay:** Smooth space combat with responsive controls and particle effects.
* **Power-up System:** 
  *  **Magnet:** Attracts nearby coins and power-ups.
  *  **Shield:** Protects the ship from incoming damage.
  *  **Energy & Star:** Boosts fire rate and enables triple-shot attacks.
  *  **Health & Max HP:** Heals the player and increases maximum health capacity.
  *  **Slow Motion:** Temporarily reduces enemy movement speed.
* **In-Game Shop:** Upgrade your laser damage and ship speed using collected coins.
* **Audio & Settings:** Full control over Music and Sound Effects (SFX) volume levels.
* **User Interface:** Interactive menus, smooth page transitions, and animated UI elements.
